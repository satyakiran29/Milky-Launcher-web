1. Privacy at a Glance & Scope

Welcome to Milky Launcher (“the App”), an Android home screen launcher developed by Krishna Vishwakarma and Satyakiran (skdevs).

This Privacy Policy sets forth clear disclosures regarding how Milky Launcher handles user data, system permissions, and third-party services in full compliance with the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement and User Data Policies. All personal configurations, home screen layouts, folders, and intruder photos are stored 100% locally on your device.

Core Promise: Milky Launcher never sells, rents, or monetizes personal data. Your launcher state remains in your device's isolated storage sandbox.

2. On-Device Architecture & Network Scope

Milky Launcher utilizes a clean modular architecture in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose:

  • Room SQLite (MilkyDatabase): Stores custom home page orders, dock slots, folders, 2x2 enlarged folders, and hidden app lists.
  • DataStore Preferences: Persists appearance themes, AMOLED pure black, Apple Glass acrylics, and icon scaling.
  • Network Access (android.permission.INTERNET): Used strictly for AdMob settings banner ads, OneSignal update alerts, Google Play Billing entitlement checks, and anonymous Firebase crash diagnostics.

3. Complete Permissions Declared

Declared in app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml for official launcher functionality:

android.permission.INTERNET Network Access
Purpose Powers AdMob banner ads in settings, OneSignal push notifications, Google Play In-App Billing, and search redirection.
Storage Transient network cache. Zero personal profiling or app usage data is uploaded by Milky Launcher.
com.android.vending.BILLING Google Play Billing 8.0.0
Purpose Processes Milky Launcher Pro upgrades and voluntary tip donations safely through Google Play.
Security All payment transactions are encrypted by Google. Milky Launcher never sees or stores financial information.
android.permission.CAMERA Optional Runtime Permission
Purpose Exclusively powers the optional "Intruder Selfie" feature in App Lock, taking a silent front snapshot after 3 failed unlock attempts.
Storage Saved strictly in private app storage (context.filesDir/intruder_photos). Never uploaded, user-deletable anytime.
android.permission.BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE Special App Access
Purpose Computes unread notification badge dots over app icons and media controls for the Music Player Compose widget.
Storage Kept strictly in volatile RAM. Never saved to persistent disk, purged immediately on dismissal.
android.permission.USE_BIOMETRIC Hardware Enclave API
Purpose Unlocks locked apps and the Hidden Apps Vault using fingerprint or face authentication.
Security Handled by Android Secure Element. Milky Launcher receives only a boolean pass/fail result.
