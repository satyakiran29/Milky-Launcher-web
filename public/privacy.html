1. Overview & Privacy Commitment

This Privacy Policy governs the use of Milky Launcher (“the Application”), an Android home screen launcher developed by Krishna Vishwakarma and Satyakiran (skdevs).

Your launcher is the front door to your personal device. We believe it should remain completely private. Milky Launcher does not collect, sell, harvest, transmit, or share any personal information, device telemetry, or usage statistics.

Core Principle: Milky Launcher is an offline utility. We operate no remote servers to receive, store, or profile your data.

2. Zero Internet & Offline Operation

Unlike many commercial launchers, Milky Launcher does not require internet connectivity:

  • No Network Permission: The Application does not declare the android.permission.INTERNET permission. Android prevents applications without this permission from making outbound network connections.
  • No Remote Databases: We have no external APIs or cloud servers connected to the launcher.
  • Web Search Delegation: When a user types a query in the app drawer search and taps web search, the search is passed directly to the device’s default web browser via standard Android intents (Intent.ACTION_WEB_SEARCH). Milky Launcher does not intercept, log, or record your query.

4. Prominent Disclosure: Camera & Intruder Selfie

Camera Usage Disclosure: The camera permission is requested solely for the optional "Intruder Selfie" security feature under App Lock.

When enabled by the user in Settings, if an unauthorized individual fails 3 consecutive unlock attempts, the front-facing camera captures a silent snapshot. The snapshot is saved locally to private internal app storage. Captured images are NEVER transmitted over any network and are completely deleted when the user clears intruder photos or uninstalls Milky Launcher.

5. Prominent Disclosure: Notification Access

Notification Access Disclosure: Notification access is requested solely to display unread count badges and notification dots on launcher icons.

Incoming notification details (package name, notification count, title, and preview snippet) are held in volatile device memory and discarded when dismissed. They are never written to disk or shared with external parties.

7. Data Retention & User Data Deletion Policy

Because all application data is stored locally on your device, you have complete ownership and control over data deletion:

  • Clear Intruder Selfies: Open Milky Launcher Settings > App Lock > Intruder Photos and tap "Clear All Photos".
  • Clear All App Data: Navigate to Android Settings > Apps > Milky Launcher > Storage > Clear Storage. This wipes all preferences, custom layouts, and databases.
  • Uninstallation: Uninstalling Milky Launcher immediately purges 100% of internal storage directories and cached data from your device.

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Milky Launcher does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). The app does not display targeted advertisements or collect age-specific data, and fully complies with COPPA and GDPR Article 8.

9. Security Architecture

All launcher settings, icon selections, and security snapshots are protected by Android's application sandbox. With zero outbound network permissions, Milky Launcher has zero external attack surface for remote data exfiltration.
